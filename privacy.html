How TapNTrust handles information used to program, fulfil and support your order.

Last updated 15 August 2026

Information we collect

  • The business name, location and review link selected to program your card.
  • The products, package and optional add-ons placed in your cart.
  • Contact, delivery and order information provided through Shopify Checkout.
  • Messages and information you choose to send when contacting support.

How we use information

We use this information to program and test products, process and deliver orders, provide customer support, prevent misuse and meet legal or accounting obligations. We do not use customer business credentials and will never ask for a Google or payment password.

Shopify Checkout

Checkout, payment and order details are handled by Shopify and its payment providers. The TapNTrust storefront does not collect or store full card numbers, CVVs or payment credentials.

Google business search

When you use business search, the search text and selected public listing information are processed using Google Maps Platform services. The chosen business name, location and review link are attached to the Shopify cart so we can program the order.

Browser storage

The storefront uses browser storage to remember the Shopify cart identifier and recover the cart on the same device. You can clear this information using your browser controls.

Storage and disclosure

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for fulfilment, support, record-keeping and legal obligations. Information may be shared with service providers such as Shopify, payment providers, Google Maps Platform and Australia Post where needed to provide the service. We do not sell personal information.

Contact and requests

For privacy questions or requests to access or correct information, email support@tapntrust.com.
